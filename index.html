Stage 02
CORE SUBSYSTEMS

Hands, Brain, and Power That Scale

What Boozy™ and Marshmallow™ prove at desk scale is the product underneath: dexterous hands, a local embodied brain, and multi-rail power. The Vallhund arm is the manipulation module on the bench beside them.

DEXTEROUS HANDS

VH-MICRO-HAND-3F → Full-Scale Hands

  • Independent serial-bus fingers: A 3-finger hand today, with each finger on the bus and a path to multi-DOF hands for full-size workcells.
  • Tactile feedback: Load on the serial bus tells the hand when a grip is real, so a person can take the object back.
  • VLA manipulation: Vision-language-action policies trained on this hand, meant to move from micro humanoids up to larger arms.
EMBODIED BRAIN

Sensory Stack, On the Machine

  • 50 Hz local ONNX: Control loops stay on the robot. No cloud round-trip in the balance or grasp loop.
  • VLA reasoning: Vision-language-action policies sit on top of that loop for manipulation, not in a remote session.
  • Voice and vision: A person can talk to the robot and be seen by it locally, without waiting on a vendor cloud.
VH-PWR-2.0

High-Density Multi-Rail Power

  • Isolated rails: Actuators, the hand bus, and compute each have their own rail so a burst of motion does not drop the brain.
  • 30A hot-swap cartridge: The pack changes while the compute rail holds.
  • Built for the load: The same architecture that runs a desk humanoid is the one we size up for larger machines.

What the Stack Is For

These are designs, not products. They show where the Boozy™ brain and hand stack can go. None of them are for sale, and none of them are in production.

CONCEPT ARCHITECTURE

Full-Scale Home & Workspace Humanoid

A concept for scaling the Marshmallow™ and Boozy™ brain and hand stack to a full-size humanoid for home and workspace assistance. Commercial. Domestic. Still on the drawing board.

CONCEPT ARCHITECTURE

VH-MARLOW Utility Quadruped

A concept quadruped for home, property, and commercial inspection. Live first-person telemetry and modular payload shells. VH-MARLOW is an R&D horizon, not a robot you can order.

R&D HORIZON

Telepresence & Sensory Cockpit

A concept for real-time 50 Hz XR and head-tracked teleoperation, plus an imitation-learning pipeline for teaching the robot a physical skill. Architecture only.
