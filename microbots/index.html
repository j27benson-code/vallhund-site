Articulated 3-Finger Micro Hands & 3-Rail Hot-Swap Power

Every Vallhund Microbots™ humanoid ships with complete upper-body manipulation and brownout-proof 3-rail power—never an armless walker chassis.

VH-MICRO-HAND-3F // TRACK B LOCKED

3-Finger Rigid 4-Bar Micro Hand

  • 2 Parallel Fingers + Opposing Thumb: 42.0 mm micro palm with rigid 25.5 mm dorsal 4-bar linkage bars (zero fragile tendon strings).
  • Track B Independent Bus Actuation: 3× Feetech SCS0009 TTL serial-bus servos per hand (Left IDs 17–19, Right IDs 25–27) on the main 1 Mbps Waveshare bus.
  • Compliant Force Handoff: Real-time bus load sensing enables 80 ms human take-over release, index-finger tool trigger pull, and expressive hand gestures.
VH-PWR-2.0 // 3-RAIL CARTRIDGE

3-Rail Brownout-Proof Power Module

  • Rail A (HV_ACT 7.4V–8.4V @ 30A): Dual Molicel INR-18650-P30B high-drain cells + low-ESR polymer bulk caps driving 14× STS3215 body/arm servos with <0.15V sag.
  • Rail B (HAND_6V Regulated 8A/12A): Dedicated synchronous step-down rail protecting all 6× SCS0009 micro-hand servos under sustained grip load.
  • Rail C (BRAIN_5V Isolated 5.1V 5A): Galvanically isolated compute, camera, audio & 8×32 RGB visor rail with ideal-diode <10s hot-swap bridge.
ON-PREMISE EMBODIED MACHINE AI

Eyes + Ears + Voice + 50 Hz ONNX Brain

  • 50 Hz Neural Locomotion: Sim-to-real MuJoCo ONNX policy + GY-BNO085 9-DOF IMU balance loop + 15-Trick Studio & Retro Gridiron #00 / #26 collector shells.
  • Local Vision & Audio Loop: NexiGo N60 1080p POV eye camera + stereo mic array running local Whisper speech recognition & person/hand tracking.
  • Zero Cloud Subscriptions: Every weight file, voice loop, and teleop cockpit runs 100% on-premise under your physical control.
